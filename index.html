Hello !

	My name is Nathanael, but online I go by Wontairr. My best work can be found in my Programming or 3D Animation projects, but I also have experience in other digital work such as 3D Modeling, Sound Design, Video Editing, and more!
	
    I have spent most of my life learning the solo game development pipeline through game engines like Unity, Godot, and Source. Overtime I've realized that while I'm quite capable by myself, my work is most enjoyable when I am part of a team. Being able to give and receive feedback on each others work is how projects get refined and become great.

    Feel free to use the buttons on the left side to navigate the site! Right below this paragraph is my portfolio.
